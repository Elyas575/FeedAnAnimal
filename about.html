About FeedAnAnimalMap

No stray should go hungry — find nearby animals and log every meal.

What this is

  • A crowdsourced map of stray cats and dogs, with feeding stations and water bowls
  • Real animals, real sightings — every feed, refill and report is logged live
  • Anyone can log a feed, refill a station, or report a stray they found

Every bowl matters

A missed meal can be serious for a street animal. Feeders move, bowls go empty, and nobody knows which regulars haven't been seen. This map closes that gap — one shared log of who ate, who needs help, and where.

How to help

  • Log your feeds and water refills, so others see the animal is looked after
  • Share with a friend who feeds strays in the neighbourhood

Keeping this running

FeedAnAnimalMap is free, but not free to run — maps, servers, and photo storage are real monthly costs. It's built and maintained by one person, currently with no salary coming in, so support goes a long way toward keeping it going.

Feed well. Help others feed well. That's the whole idea.

Anything beyond costs goes to our giving: at least 10% of profits goes to causes we care about — the ASPCA and Humane Society. When there are profits to report, we'll publish the numbers.
