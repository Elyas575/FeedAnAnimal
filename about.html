`StrayFeedingMap

About

This platform was created to help people find and feed street animals in their area, making it easier to find and care for animals in need.

What is this?

  • A live, crowdsourced map of stray cats, dogs, or any others.
  • Real feeders logging real meals, caring for real animals together.

Feed a Life

Three small actions keep the map alive:

  • Post an animal if you spot a stray in your area
  • Log a meal whenever you leave food
  • Check the map to see where help is needed today

How to use it

  1. Allow location click "Allow" when the browser asks.
  2. Log in sign in to your account.
  3. Report a stray tap the orange button, upload a photo, tick what they need, save.
  4. Feed or water tap any pin, then "I Fed" or "I Watered".
  5. Done! the pin updates for everyone.

Keeping this running

StrayFeedingMap is free for everyone. Hosting, map tiles, and storage cost real money built and maintained by one person.

Frequently asked questions

What do I feed a stray cat? +

Stray cats do best with wet or dry cat food avoid giving them milk, as most cats are lactose intolerant. Fresh water is just as important. If you don't have cat food, plain cooked chicken or fish (no bones, no seasoning) works in a pinch.

Learn more: ASPCA Cat Care Guide · Humane Society Feeding Stray Cats

What do I feed a stray dog? +

Stray dogs can eat dry or wet dog food, or plain cooked rice with boiled chicken (no spices, no bones). Always provide clean water. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, and salty foods they're toxic to dogs.

Learn more: ASPCA Dog Care Guide · Humane Society Feeding Stray Dogs

Is it safe to approach a stray animal? +

Approach slowly and calmly. Let the animal come to you don't chase or corner it. If the animal seems aggressive or fearful, keep your distance and contact a local animal rescue or shelter.

Learn more: Humane Society What to Do If You Find a Stray

How often should I feed a stray? +

If you can, leave food and water daily at the same spot and time. Strays quickly learn routines. Even feeding once a day makes a big difference for their health and survival.

Learn more: Alley Cat Allies Feeding Community Cats
